Why Track Safety Incidents?

Safety incident tracking is a critical component of occupational health and safety management. By recording all incidents—from minor near-misses to serious accidents—organizations can identify patterns, implement preventive measures, and create safer work environments.

Severity Levels

  • Low: Minor incidents with no or minimal injury (near-misses, minor cuts)
  • Medium: Moderate incidents requiring first aid or medical attention (minor fractures, burns)
  • High: Serious incidents with significant injury or potential for serious injury (hospitalizable injuries, fatalities)

Incident Types

The SafetyIncident table stores records of various incident types including falls, electrical hazards, equipment failures, and environmental hazards. Each incident is linked to a specific project (Area) for easy compliance tracking and risk assessment.
